Privacy Policy for Focus Timer

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Focus Timer (package io.github.shiv8080.focus_timer) does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

  • The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Your timer settings and session counts are saved only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • The app requests no Android permissions and does not use the internet.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/shiv8080/focus-timer/issues.
