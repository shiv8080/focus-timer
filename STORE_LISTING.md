**Full description** (max 4000 chars)

Focus Timer helps you work in focused sessions with regular breaks, using the Pomodoro technique.

• Focus, short-break and long-break timers
• Adjust each length: focus 5–90 min, breaks 1–60 min
• Choose how many focus sessions come before a long break
• Optional auto-start of the next timer
• See how many focus sessions you've finished today
• The timer stays accurate if you leave the app
• Light and dark themes that follow your system
• No ads, no account, no permissions, no data collection

How it works:
1. Press start and focus on one task until the timer ends.
2. Take a short break.
3. After a few sessions, take a longer break.
